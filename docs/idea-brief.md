# Idea brief — pixelmove

## 1. Raw idea

я роблю фото та відео на Iphone Pro 15 бо хочу продати на Shuterstock. я хочу зберігати орігінальний матеріал на GOOGLE photos. робити загрузку : Iphone-> Pixel1->GOOGLE photos

## 2. Problem

The author needs to preserve original iPhone 15 Pro photos and videos intended for sale on Shutterstock without paying for large cloud storage volumes. Monthly batches may contain 200–300 photos, plus an unspecified volume of video. Before uploading, the author wants to compare duplicate and similar photos and remove unwanted originals.

## 3. Users

The initial user is the idea author, a photographer and video creator using an iPhone 15 Pro, a Pixel 1, and Google Photos. Material will be selected and transferred by calendar month and year. Supporting other creators is not the chosen initial direction.

## 4. Why now

The author has already transferred one photo through Pixel 1 and wants to extend this trial into a usable personal workflow. No deadline or other external trigger was stated.

## 5. Out of scope

- A product for other creators: the author chose a personal application.
- Fully automatic background transfer: the author chose an explicit single-action launch.
- Automatic duplicate deletion: the author reviews suggested groups and confirms deletion.
- Automatic removal of retained originals after upload: these remain on iPhone and are deleted manually if desired.
- Defining Shutterstock preparation and submission in the first stage: the broader goal includes selection and preparation for sale, but the initial focus is selection, transfer, and original storage; later capabilities remain undecided.

## 6. Risks

- Weakest spot: the desktop application must reliably associate each reviewed photo with its iPhone original before issuing a confirmed deletion. Access and recovery behavior are unverified.
- Similarity groups may contain distinct valuable shots; the system proposes groups, while the author chooses what to delete.
- One transferred photo does not establish reliable monthly transfer of 200–300 photos and videos, preservation of originals, or recovery after interruptions.
- The workflow assumes Pixel 1 remains available and uploads qualify for free original-quality storage. Google currently documents unlimited original-quality storage at no charge for first-generation Pixel, but the transferred iPhone files still need practical verification. Source: https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/6220791 (checked 2026-10-03).
- Video count, duration, size, and formats are unknown, so capacity and transfer time cannot yet be established.

## 7. Recommendation

Build a personal application used on a computer: select a calendar month and year, review exact duplicates and near-identical photos together on one screen, and confirm deletion of unwanted iPhone originals. Start transfer of the retained photos and videos with one explicit action through iPhone → Pixel 1 → Google Photos, with a visible result indicating saved items and items requiring another attempt. Retained iPhone originals remain after uploading; the author handles any later removal manually. Validate this initial workflow before expanding into preparation for Shutterstock.

## 8. Open questions

- Owner: idea author — How was the first photo transferred, and which parts of that process are already configured?
- Owner: idea author — Which computer operating system will host the review interface?
- Owner: idea author — What photo and video formats are used, and what are typical monthly video counts, durations, and sizes?
- Owner: implementation lead — How can the application access iPhone originals and perform author-confirmed deletion, and how will recovery work?
- Owner: idea author + implementation lead — What verification is sufficient to mark an original as safely stored, including quality, metadata, and applicable storage conditions?
- Owner: idea author + implementation lead — How will interrupted transfers, repeat runs, and already uploaded files be handled?
- Owner: idea author — Should videos be reviewed for duplicates as well? Only photo grouping was requested.
- Owner: idea author — What selection, editing, description, keyword, and submission assistance is wanted for the later Shutterstock stage?
